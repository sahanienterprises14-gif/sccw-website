About SCCW

Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane & Panvel Ka Trusted Construction Partner

Sahani Civil Construction Works (SCCW) is a premier civil engineering and contracting firm proudly serving Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Panvel & Pune. With experienced engineers and a highly skilled workforce, we deliver premium finishing and modern construction methods to every project.
